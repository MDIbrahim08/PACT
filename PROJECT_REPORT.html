1. Project Overview & Executive Summary

Official Problem Statement: Transforming informal commercial communication (WhatsApp messages, telephone conversations, and regional voice notes) into legally enforceable, court-admissible commercial contracts for Indian MSMEs under the Indian Contract Act 1872 and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023.

Executive Summary: Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) drive 30% of India's GDP, with over 63 million enterprises operating in wholesale clusters such as Surat (textiles), Moradabad (brassware), and Delhi (packaging). Over 90% of B2B transactions are negotiated through casual WhatsApp chats and Hinglish voice notes. Traditional 10-page lawyer agreements destroy social trust ("Aapko hum par vishwas nahi hai kya?"), cost ₹3,000–₹15,000 per deal, and require English literacy. Consequently, traders operate on handshakes, leading to an estimated ₹10.7 Lakh Crore delayed payments crisis.

PACT acts as an invisible, neutral commercial notary. Operating across web, mobile PWA, standalone desktop, and direct WhatsApp auto-pilot, PACT ingests vernacular voice notes, verifies the 5 statutory pillars under Section 10 of the Indian Contract Act 1872, audits against Section 29 void uncertainty, incorporates MSMED Act 2006 Section 15 (45-day payment ceiling & 3x RBI interest), and mints court-admissible electronic evidence certificates sealed with dual-node SHA-256 digests in strict accordance with Section 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

2. Problem Understanding & The MSME Reality

In wholesale markets across India, deals occur via quick voice notes and WhatsApp exchanges. However, when disputes arise:

  • Hearsay & Inadmissibility: Raw WhatsApp screenshots are easily fabricated. Under Section 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA), electronic records require a cryptographic certificate signed by the device custodian.
  • Section 29 Invalidation: Vague promises like "Diwali ke baad hisaab karenge" void agreements for uncertainty.
  • Credit Default: Without verifiable proof of agreed delivery dates, MSMEs cannot recover payments through MSME Samadhaan arbitration councils.

5. Technical Strategy & Data Flow

PACT operates as a dual-node system: the client generates a preliminary SHA-256 digest locally via window.crypto.subtle; upon server dispatch, Node.js computes an authoritative HMAC-SHA256 digest verified using crypto.timingSafeEqual() to prevent side-channel timing attacks.

10. Limitations and Honest Disclosure

11. Real-World Viability, Security & Privacy

Zero-Storage Guarantee: Conversation logs and private transaction data are processed strictly in temporary session memory and client-side IndexedDB caches. No private chats are stored on remote database servers. If uninstalled, all local keys are purged.
