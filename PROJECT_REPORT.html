1. Project Overview

Official Problem Statement: Transforming informal commercial communication (WhatsApp messages, telephone calls, and regional voice notes) into legally enforceable, court-admissible contracts for Indian MSMEs under the Indian Contract Act 1872 and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023.

Executive Summary: Over 90% of micro, small, and medium enterprise (MSME) transactions in India are agreed upon via informal WhatsApp chats and Hinglish voice notes without written agreements. Traditional 10-page legal contracts destroy commercial trust ("Aapko hum par vishwas nahi hai kya?"), cost thousands of rupees, and require English literacy. Consequently, Indian small businesses suffer from a catastrophic ₹10.7 Lakh Crore delayed payments crisis.

PACT is an invisible, neutral AI notary and commercial assistant. Ingesting informal voice notes or chats, PACT validates the 5 statutory pillars under Section 10 of the Indian Contract Act 1872, eliminates ambiguities that void agreements under Section 29, incorporates MSMED Act 2006 statutory 45-day payment limits, mints court-admissible electronic evidence certificates sealed with SHA-256 digests under BSA 2023 §63(4), and auto-launches WhatsApp with stamped notices pre-filled.

2. Problem Understanding

Target Users: Textile manufacturers (Surat), packaging hubs, grain merchants, brassware artisans (Moradabad), independent B2B suppliers, and MSME legal advocates.

  • The Social Friction Paradox: Demanding a 10-page advocate contract signals mistrust, alienating long-term wholesale counterparties.
  • Judicial Inadmissibility: Raw WhatsApp screenshots fail in Indian courts because Section 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (formerly Section 65B of the Evidence Act) mandates an electronic evidence certificate with cryptographic hash verification and custodian declarations.
  • Void for Uncertainty (Section 29 ICA 1872): Vague verbal terms like "payment delivery ke baad dekh lenge" render agreements legally unenforceable in commercial courts.
  • The Delayed Payment Trap: Despite Section 15 of the MSMED Act 2006 mandating a 45-day payment cap, small businesses have no stamped document to present before MSME Samadhaan facilitation councils.

How PACT Works: PACT listens to everyday Hinglish/vernacular deal terms, extracts Section 10 contract pillars, applies a "Blame-the-Bot" social nudge to clarify missing terms without offending the buyer, seals the contract with dual-node SHA-256 cryptographic digests, and opens WhatsApp automatically with a court-ready commercial notice.
  • Vernacular Voice Deal Notary: Speaks and transcribes in Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Gujarati, and English via Sarvam AI (saaras:v3) and ElevenLabs ultra-fast foreign voiceout.
  • Section 10 Deterministic Validator: Zero-hallucination verification of competent parties, free consent, lawful consideration, and definite objects.
  • MSMED Act 2006 Enforcement: Injects statutory 45-day payment ceilings and Section 16 compounding interest penalties (3x the RBI bank rate) on overdue invoices.
  • BSA 2023 §63(4) Evidence Seal: Computes an immutable SHA-256 digital fingerprint across canonical deal terms to prevent single-character screenshot tampering.
  • Government e-Stamp Non-Judicial Preview: Renders authentic printable Indian non-judicial stamp papers with state codes, dual QR verification, and notary endorsements.
  • WhatsApp Auto-Pilot & Named Contact Routing: Direct auto-launching of WhatsApp chats with pre-filled contracts for named counterparties (Salman Khan, Prabhanjan More, Mohammed Ibrahim, Rajesh Patel).

5. System Architecture

PACT employs a 4-tier dual-node architecture balancing client-side privacy with server-side cryptographic signing:

  • Tier 1: Client Layer (PWA / Browser / Standalone): Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS, AIChatCard typewriter interface, and W3C WebCrypto API (window.crypto.subtle). Operates offline in low-connectivity godowns.
  • Tier 2: Server Gateway (Node.js 20+): Secure API routes (/api/analyze-deal, /api/chat, /api/transcribe, /api/tts) protected by enterprise security headers (CSP, HSTS).
  • Tier 3: Cryptographic & Statutory Engine: Canonical UTF-8 normalization, SHA-256 hashing, and crypto.timingSafeEqual() verification to neutralize side-channel attacks.
  • Tier 4: Telephony & Dispatch Channels: Deep-linked WhatsApp automation API, native SMS telephony gateway, and print-isolated e-Stamp generator.
